Frequently Asked Questions

Ordering Delivery Payment Replacement Policy

Ordering

How do I place an order?

Browse any collection, add items to your cart, then check out either by paying online or via bank transfer — or skip the cart entirely and order a specific item straight through WhatsApp using the "Order on WhatsApp" button on any product page.

Can I change or cancel my order after placing it?

Yes, as long as it hasn't been shipped yet. Message us on WhatsApp with your order details as soon as possible and we'll do our best to adjust or cancel it.

Do you offer bulk or hospitality (B2B) pricing?

Yes — for hotels, short-lets, and bulk orders, reach out to us directly on WhatsApp or by email for custom pricing.

Delivery

Which states do you deliver to?

We deliver nationwide — all 36 states and the FCT, Nigeria.

How long does delivery take?

Lagos and Abuja typically arrive within 1–3 business days. Other states usually take 3–7 business days depending on location. You'll receive delivery updates via WhatsApp once your order ships.

Is there a delivery fee?

Delivery cost depends on your location and is calculated at checkout before you confirm payment.

Payment

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept card and bank transfer payments through Flutterwave, as well as direct bank transfer with proof-of-payment upload at checkout.

Is it safe to pay online on this site?

Yes. Card payments are processed securely by Flutterwave — COZY-LUXE never sees or stores your card details.

Replacement Policy

What is your replacement policy?

We do not offer refunds. However, if you receive a damaged item, kindly notify us within 48 hours of delivery with clear photos. Eligible items will be replaced after verification.

What counts as a "damaged item"?

Items that arrive stained, torn, structurally faulty, or otherwise not in sellable condition. Please photograph the item and its packaging as soon as you notice the issue.

How do I request a replacement?

Message us on WhatsApp within 48 hours of delivery with your order details and clear photos of the damage. Once verified, we'll arrange a replacement at no extra cost.
